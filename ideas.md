<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Neumorphism (Soft UI)
  **Core Principles**:
  1.  **Softness & Depth**: Elements appear to be extruded from the background, creating a tactile feel.
  2.  **Minimalism**: Focus on essential data and controls, reducing visual clutter.
  3.  **Comfort**: A calming, stress-free environment for financial planning.
  **Color Philosophy**:
  *   **Base**: Light, warm gray or off-white (e.g., `#E0E5EC`) to serve as the "material".
  *   **Highlights/Shadows**: White for highlights, darker gray for shadows to create the 3D effect.
  *   **Accents**: Soft pastel blue or green for positive financial indicators (growth, freedom).
  **Layout Paradigm**:
  *   **Card-Based Flow**: Each input section (Current Balance, Growth, etc.) lives on its own "soft" card.
  *   **Central Visualization**: The graph is the hero, embedded deeply into the surface.
  **Signature Elements**:
  1.  **Soft Buttons**: Buttons that look like they can be physically pressed (inner shadows on active state).
  2.  **Inset Inputs**: Input fields that appear carved into the surface.
  3.  **Rounded Corners**: Heavy use of border-radius to enhance the soft, organic feel.
  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  *   **Tactile Feedback**: Visual states that mimic physical buttons (pressed vs. unpressed).
  *   **Smooth Transitions**: Gentle easing for all movements.
  **Animation**:
  *   **Slow & Steady**: Graphs draw slowly to represent the passage of time.
  *   **Float**: subtle hovering effects for interactive elements.
  **Typography System**:
  *   **Headings**: `Nunito` (Rounded sans-serif) for a friendly, approachable vibe.
  *   **Body**: `Quicksand` or `Varela Round` to maintain the soft geometry.
</idea>
</text>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Swiss Style (International Typographic Style)
  **Core Principles**:
  1.  **Grid Systems**: Strict adherence to a mathematical grid for order and clarity.
  2.  **Objective Photography/Graphics**: Use of abstract geometric shapes to represent financial growth.
  3.  **Asymmetry**: Dynamic layouts that avoid centering everything.
  **Color Philosophy**:
  *   **Base**: Stark White (`#FFFFFF`) for maximum contrast.
  *   **Accents**: Bold, primary colors (Red `#FF3B30`, Blue `#007AFF`) used sparingly but effectively to guide the eye.
  *   **Text**: Deep Black (`#000000`) for crisp readability.
  **Layout Paradigm**:
  *   **Split Screen**: Inputs on the left (fixed), dynamic visualization on the right (scrollable or sticky).
  *   **Typographic Hierarchy**: Large, bold headings that act as structural elements.
  **Signature Elements**:
  1.  **Thick Dividers**: Bold black lines separating sections.
  2.  **Oversized Numbers**: Key metrics (Years to FIRE, Total Amount) displayed in massive font sizes.
  3.  **Geometric Icons**: Simple, solid shapes.
  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  *   **Direct & Snappy**: Instant updates with zero lag.
  *   **Hover States**: High contrast changes (e.g., black button turns white with black border).
  **Animation**:
  *   **Sharp Cuts**: Minimal fading, prefer sliding or scaling with snappy easing.
  **Typography System**:
  *   **Headings**: `Helvetica Now` or `Inter` (Bold/Black weights) for authoritative presence.
  *   **Body**: `Roboto Mono` for data and numbers to emphasize precision.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Glassmorphism (Frosted Glass)
  **Core Principles**:
  1.  **Translucency**: Background blur to create a sense of depth and context.
  2.  **Vivid Backgrounds**: Colorful, abstract orbs or gradients floating behind the glass layers.
  3.  **Light Borders**: Subtle white borders to define edges of glass panes.
  **Color Philosophy**:
  *   **Background**: Deep, rich gradient (e.g., Midnight Blue to Purple) representing the "future" or "universe" of possibilities.
  *   **Glass**: White with low opacity (10-20%) and background blur.
  *   **Text**: White for high contrast against the dark/vivid background.
  **Layout Paradigm**:
  *   **Floating Layers**: Content sits on floating glass panes above the background.
  *   **Z-Axis Hierarchy**: Most important elements (Result) are "closer" to the user (more opaque, brighter).
  **Signature Elements**:
  1.  **Frosted Cards**: The main container for the calculator.
  2.  **Glowing Orbs**: Background elements that move slowly.
  3.  **Gradient Text**: For the main title "FIRE Calculator".
  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  *   **Ethereal**: Interactions feel weightless and fluid.
  *   **Glow Effects**: Elements glow when hovered or active.
  **Animation**:
  *   **Parallax**: Background moves slightly differently than foreground on scroll.
  *   **Shimmer**: Glass surfaces shimmer when data updates.
  **Typography System**:
  *   **Headings**: `Outfit` or `Space Grotesk` for a modern, tech-forward look.
  *   **Body**: `DM Sans` for clean readability on transparent backgrounds.
</idea>
